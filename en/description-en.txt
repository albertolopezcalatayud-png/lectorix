Lectorix is an app for practising reading and writing every day with short activities. It is designed especially for primary and secondary school students with difficulties such as dyslexia, dysgraphia, or problems with reading comprehension or phonological awareness.

🧭 START AT YOUR LEVEL
A short test places each student at the right level in each area. The activities adapt to how they answer: if something is hard, it comes back for review; if it goes well, the difficulty goes up.

📖 GUIDED READING
Texts light up word by word as they are read aloud. Choose the speed and tap any word to hear it. Comprehension questions follow. Students pick their favourite topics and the app suggests readings at their level.

🧚 STORIES MADE FOR YOU
Students choose a hero, a place and an object, and the app writes a story at their level using their own tricky words.

📷 ADAPT MY HOMEWORK
Take a photo of a page from a book or share a text from another app, and read it with coloured syllables, extra spacing, a reading ruler and a voice. It also creates questions to study that text.

🎯 UNDERSTAND EXAM QUESTIONS
What “define”, “explain”, “compare”, “justify” and other exam verbs ask for, with model answers. Paste a question and the app splits it into tasks so none get missed.

📝 PLAN YOUR WRITING
Opinion essays, stories, descriptions and letters, step by step: ideas, paragraphs with linking words, and a final check of length, long sentences, repetition, punctuation and spelling.

✍️ WRITE AND LISTEN
Students write, listen to what they have written and check their spelling offline, with suggestions for common mix-ups and word predictions as they type.

🎧 DICTATIONS
Hundreds of word and sentence dictations for the most common mix-ups. If there is a mistake, the app shows word by word what is right and what is missing.

🧩 FIVE AREAS
Phonology, visual memory, reading comprehension, writing and quick challenges: seven levels in English (Pre-A1 to B1+) and nine in Spanish and Valencian. Over 1,300 activities in each language.

📚 MY BOOKSHELF AND READING CHALLENGE
Log the paper books you read, and take part in a weekly reading challenge with family or friends, with no accounts or chat.

🎤 READING ALOUD (OPTIONAL)
If the family turns it on, students read aloud and the app marks the words it recognises. It uses the device’s own speech recognition, offline. The voice is never recorded or stored.

♿ ACCESSIBILITY
Optional OpenDyslexic font, adjustable text size and spacing, high-contrast mode and read-aloud with the device’s voice.

⭐ MOTIVATION
Stars, points, achievements, daily streaks, a daily challenge and an owl mascot. Optional reminders.

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR FAMILIES
A weekly summary in plain language (also in Arabic, Romanian, Ukrainian, Chinese and Urdu), a skills map, a downloadable report, printable worksheets with answers and a guide to supporting reading at home.

🔒 PRIVACY
No ads and no trackers. Progress is saved on the device and no account is needed.

🌍 LANGUAGES
British English, Spanish and Valencian, each with its own activities, dictations and readings.

Trying it costs nothing. Optional subscription plans will come later.

Lectorix is a practice and support tool: it is not a treatment, it does not diagnose and it does not replace assessment or support from a professional (speech therapist, school counsellor or educational psychologist).
