Word lists for the offline spelling check of Lectorix ("Escribe y escúchalo").
They are derived from these Hunspell dictionaries (all word forms expanded, words with
hyphens or apostrophes removed, front-coded and gzipped (.fcz: gzip data; not named .gz because Android packaging renames .gz files) by scripts/spell/build.py):

es.fcz  Spanish dictionary (es_ES), RLA-ES project, Santiago Bosio and contributors.
          License: GPL-3+ or LGPL-3+ or MPL-1.1+ (used here under LGPL-3+).
          https://github.com/sbosio/rla-es

ca.fcz  Catalan dictionary, Valencian variant (ca_ES-valencia), Softcatalà,
          Joan Moratinos, Jaume Ortolà and contributors. License: GPL-2+ and LGPL-2.1+
          (used here under LGPL-2.1+). https://github.com/Softcatala/catalan-dict-tools

en.fcz  British English dictionary (en_GB), David Bartlett, Andrew Brown,
          Marco A.G. Pinto and contributors. License: LGPL.
          https://github.com/marcoagpinto/aoo-mozilla-en-dict

These files may be replaced by modified versions; the app only reads them as data.
